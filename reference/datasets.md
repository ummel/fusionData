# Internal datasets available in fusionData

An overview of internal reference datasets bundled with the package.

## Details

- [`bg_centroids_2010`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/bg_centroids_2010.md):

  Population-weighted block group centroids (2010 Census).

- [`bg_centroids_2020`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/bg_centroids_2020.md):

  Population-weighted block group centroids (2020 Census).

- [`bg_crosswalk`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/bg_crosswalk.md):

  Geographic crosswalk between 2010 and 2020 block groups.

- [`BEA_pce_national`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/BEA_pce_national.md):

  BEA national Personal Consumption Expenditures series (1959–2020).

- [`BEA_pce_state`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/BEA_pce_state.md):

  BEA state-level Personal Consumption Expenditures series (1997–2019).

- [`cpi_series`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/cpi_series.md):

  Annual Consumer Price Index series from FRED.

- [`poverty_thresholds`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/poverty_thresholds.md):

  Historical official U.S. Census Bureau poverty thresholds.

- [`puma_crosswalk`](https://ummel.github.io/fusionData/reference/puma_crosswalk.md):

  Geographic crosswalk between 2010 and 2020 PUMAs.
